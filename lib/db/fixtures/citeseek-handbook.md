# Northwind Remote Work Handbook

Policies for employees working remotely at Northwind Ltd. Last reviewed March 2026. Questions
about anything not covered here go to your line manager.

## Expenses and reimbursement

Employees may claim reimbursement for equipment, software licenses and co-working space.
Claims must be submitted within 60 days of purchase, and **reimbursement is paid within 30
days of an approved claim**. Claims over £500 require written approval from a line manager
before the purchase is made, not after.

Receipts are required for every claim regardless of value. A bank statement is not a receipt.
Claims submitted without a receipt are returned rather than rejected, and the 60-day window
pauses while a claim sits with the employee.

## Equipment

Every remote employee receives a laptop, an external monitor and a desk chair. The company
retains ownership of equipment for three years, after which it transfers to the employee at
no cost. Equipment that fails within the three-year period is replaced rather than repaired.

Employees may choose between a 14-inch and a 16-inch laptop. The choice is made once at
onboarding and cannot be changed until the three-year refresh.

## Working hours and availability

There are no fixed working hours. Employees are asked to overlap with their team for at least
four hours per day, and to record those overlap hours in the shared calendar. Meetings
scheduled outside a colleague's recorded overlap hours may be declined without explanation.

Response time expectations are one working day for messages and three working days for
document review. Nothing in this handbook requires a response outside working hours.

## Time off

Annual leave is 28 days plus public holidays, and unused leave does not carry over. Leave
requests of five days or fewer are approved automatically. Longer requests need a line
manager's approval and at least three weeks' notice.

Sick leave is not counted against annual leave and does not require a medical certificate for
absences of seven days or fewer.

## Data handling

Company documents may not be uploaded to third-party services that have not been approved by
the security team. The approved list is maintained in the internal wiki and reviewed
quarterly. Personal accounts may not be used for company work, including cloud storage and
note-taking applications.

Laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled. This is checked automatically at onboarding
and cannot be disabled.
