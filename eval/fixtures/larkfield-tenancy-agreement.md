# Larkfield Property — Residential Tenancy Agreement

Standard terms. Clause numbering is stable across versions; the version date is
on the signature page.

## 1. Rent

Rent is payable monthly in advance on the first day of each month. Payment by
standing order is required; card and cash payments are not accepted, and a
transfer arriving without the tenancy reference will be returned rather than
allocated.

Rent is reviewed once per year on the anniversary of the tenancy start date. Any
increase is capped at the lower of the consumer price index or 4%, and requires
two months' written notice. A review that is not served in time cannot be applied
retrospectively.

## 2. Deposit

The deposit is five weeks' rent and is registered with an approved protection
scheme within 30 days of receipt. The scheme reference is provided in writing.

Deductions may be made for unpaid rent, damage beyond fair wear and tear, and
professional cleaning where the property was professionally cleaned at the start
of the tenancy. Deductions may not be made for redecoration attributable to
ordinary use, and a dispute goes to the scheme's adjudicator rather than to court.

## 3. Repairs

The landlord is responsible for the structure and exterior, the installations for
water, gas, electricity and sanitation, and the heating system. The tenant is
responsible for internal decoration, replacing light bulbs and smoke alarm
batteries, and keeping drains and gutters accessible.

Emergency repairs are attended within 24 hours. An emergency means loss of heat
in winter, loss of water, an unsecured entrance, or anything presenting a risk to
health. Non-emergency repairs are attended within 14 days.

A tenant may arrange an emergency repair themselves if the landlord cannot be
reached within four hours, and recover the reasonable cost against rent, provided
the invoice is produced.

## 4. Access

The landlord requires 24 hours' written notice before entering, except in an
emergency where entry may be immediate. Notice must state a purpose. A tenant may
refuse a specific time and propose another within seven days, and repeated
refusal without an alternative is a breach.

Viewings during the final two months require the same notice and may not exceed
two per week.

## 5. Ending the tenancy

After the fixed term the tenancy continues monthly. The tenant gives one month's
notice ending on a rent payment date; the landlord gives two months'.

Notice must be in writing. A notice served by message or spoken in person has no
effect, which protects both parties from a disagreement about what was said.

## 6. Subletting and guests

Subletting is prohibited without written consent, which will not be unreasonably
withheld for a replacement tenant of equivalent standing. Guests staying more
than 14 consecutive nights require notification, and more than 28 nights
constitutes occupation and requires consent.

## 7. Pets

Pets require written consent, which is granted by default for one cat or one dog
in properties with private outdoor space. Consent carries an additional deposit
of one week's rent, which is registered under the same scheme and returned on the
same terms.
