# Meridian Software — Customer Support Policy

Version 4.2. Applies to all support plans sold after 1 January 2026.

## Response and resolution targets

Support tickets are triaged into four severities. Severity 1 means a production
system is unavailable to all users. Severity 2 means a production system is
degraded or unavailable to some users. Severity 3 covers defects with a
workaround. Severity 4 covers questions, documentation gaps and feature requests.

First response targets are measured from ticket creation, not from the start of
business hours: 30 minutes for Severity 1, two hours for Severity 2, one business
day for Severity 3, and three business days for Severity 4.

Resolution targets are deliberately not contractual for Severity 3 and 4. A
defect with a workaround is scheduled against the release train rather than
against a clock, and promising otherwise would mean either missing the promise or
shipping a rushed fix.

## Coverage hours

Standard plans are covered 09:00 to 18:00 in the customer's registered timezone,
Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in that country. Premier plans are
covered continuously, including weekends and holidays.

Severity 1 tickets raised outside covered hours on a Standard plan are queued and
begin their 30-minute clock when coverage resumes. This is the single most
common source of dispute, so it is stated twice: the clock does not run overnight
on a Standard plan.

## Escalation

Any ticket may be escalated once by the customer without justification. A second
escalation requires a named business impact. Escalation moves the ticket to the
duty lead, who owns it until closure and may not reassign it back to the original
queue.

Escalated Severity 1 tickets page the duty engineer directly. Escalation of a
Severity 3 or 4 ticket does not change its severity; it changes who is watching
it. Customers frequently expect otherwise, and support staff are instructed to
say so explicitly rather than let the expectation stand.

## Credits

Missing a first response target on a Premier plan credits 5% of the monthly fee
per missed target, capped at 25% in any calendar month. Standard plans carry no
service credits. Credits are applied automatically to the next invoice and do not
require the customer to claim them.

Credits are the only remedy for a missed target. They are not a refund and cannot
be exchanged for one.

## Data handling during support

Support engineers may request logs, configuration files and database schemas.
They may not request production data extracts, and a customer offering one will
be redirected to a redacted sample. Anything a customer does send is deleted from
the ticket system 90 days after closure.

Screen sharing is permitted with the customer driving. A support engineer never
takes control of a customer machine, which removes an entire category of dispute
about what was changed and by whom.

## Ending a support plan

Support plans renew annually unless cancelled 30 days before the renewal date.
Cancellation does not close open tickets: they run to resolution under the plan
in force when they were raised.

Downgrading from Premier to Standard takes effect at renewal, never mid-term,
because the coverage-hours difference would otherwise change the target on
tickets already in flight.
