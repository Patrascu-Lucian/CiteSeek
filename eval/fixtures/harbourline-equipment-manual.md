# Harbourline HL-90 Bench Press — Operating Manual

Serial range 4400–5200. Read the safety section before first use.

## Safety

The press develops 90 kilonewtons. The guard interlock is not a convenience
feature: the ram will not descend with the guard raised, and defeating the
interlock voids both the warranty and the machine's CE marking.

Hearing protection is required within two metres during operation. The press
itself measures 84 dB at one metre, which is below the mandatory threshold, but
workpiece resonance routinely adds 10 to 15 dB and is not predictable from the
material alone.

Never adjust the die while the hydraulic system is pressurized. Depressurize
using the bleed valve on the left column and confirm the gauge reads zero before
opening the guard. A gauge reading zero with the pump running indicates a failed
sensor, not a depressurized system.

## Daily checks

Before the first cycle of each shift, confirm the oil level sits between the two
marks on the sight glass, the emergency stop returns to its detent when twisted,
and the guard interlock stops the ram at any point in its travel.

Record the checks in the machine log. An unrecorded check is treated as a check
that did not happen, which matters when a fault is investigated.

## Hydraulic oil

Use ISO VG 46 mineral oil. Synthetic alternatives are compatible but change the
warm-up behavior: the press reaches working viscosity in about four minutes on
mineral oil and about ninety seconds on synthetic, and the ram will feel abrupt
during the difference.

Change the oil every 2,000 operating hours or annually, whichever comes first.
The filter is changed at the same interval and is not cleanable — a filter that
looks clean has usually failed open.

Oil temperature above 70°C shortens seal life sharply. The press will run at
higher temperatures without complaint, which is the problem: nothing warns the
operator, and the first sign is a seal failure some months later.

## Die changes

Dies are held by four M16 bolts torqued to 210 Nm in a diagonal sequence.
Retorque after the first ten cycles of a new die, because the seating face
beds in and the initial torque is no longer accurate.

A die that has been reground below 38 mm thickness must not be refitted. The
clamping geometry assumes a minimum stack height, and a thin die shifts the load
onto the bolts rather than the seating face.

## Fault codes

E01 indicates low oil level. E02 indicates the guard interlock is open. E04
indicates over-temperature and latches until the oil falls below 60°C — it will
not clear on a power cycle, which is deliberate and frequently mistaken for a
fault in the controller.

E07 indicates a pressure sensor disagreement: the two sensors differ by more than
5 bar. The press stops and must not be restarted until the disagreement is
resolved, because there is no way to know which sensor is lying.

## Storage and transport

Retract the ram fully before transport. A press moved with the ram extended will
usually arrive with a bent guide rod, and the rod is not a field-replaceable
part.

For storage over three months, coat the exposed rod in a corrosion inhibitor and
leave the guard closed. Storage humidity above 60% requires a desiccant in the
control cabinet regardless of duration.
